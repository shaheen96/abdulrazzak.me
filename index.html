About

I make the picture, then I finish it.

I am a photographer and videographer based in Dubai. Since 2021 I have shot for healthcare, hospitality and entertainment brands across the UAE — corporate portraits, facility and location work, product, and full event coverage.

I set up my own camera, lighting and audio, and I take everything through post myself: culling, retouching, colour correction, grading, sound and export to whatever the destination needs. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design and a Multimedia Journalism diploma, which is the reason I think about resolution, colour and viewing distance before I press the shutter, not after.

  • Shooting DSLR · drone · gimbal · studio lighting
  • Post Lightroom · Photoshop · Premiere Pro · After Effects
  • Languages Arabic (native) · English (professional)
